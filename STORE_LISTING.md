# Store Listing Text

## Google Play Store

### App Name
Steady Hand - Livestock Manager

### Short Description (80 chars max)
Track livestock, breeding cycles, health records, and production for your farm.

### Full Description
Steady Hand is the complete livestock management app designed for homesteaders, hobby farmers, and small-scale ranchers. Track your entire herd or flock from birth to market with comprehensive record-keeping tools.

ANIMAL MANAGEMENT
- Track unlimited animals across 15+ species including cattle, goats, sheep, pigs, chickens, horses, rabbits, and more
- Record essential details: names, tag IDs, breeds, birth dates, and lineage
- Monitor animal status: Active, Sold, Deceased, or Transferred
- Search and filter your inventory by species, status, or name
- Add custom fields for species-specific information

COMPLETE EVENT TRACKING
- Log health events: vaccinations, treatments, vet visits, deworming, and hoof trimming
- Record breeding events: heat observations, breeding dates, pregnancy checks, and births
- Track production: milk yields, egg collection, and fiber harvests
- Monitor weight and growth over time
- View complete event history for each animal

SMART REMINDERS
- Automatic reminders based on species-specific breeding cycles and gestation periods
- Never miss a vaccination, pregnancy check, or expected birth date
- Set custom recurring reminders for routine tasks
- Push notifications keep you informed

CALENDAR VIEW
- See all events and reminders at a glance
- Navigate easily between months
- View daily task lists with animal details

REPORTS AND ANALYTICS
- Generate inventory, breeding, health, production, and weight reports
- Export data to CSV for record-keeping or compliance
- Track trends and performance over time

DATA SECURITY
- Create full backups of your farm data
- Restore from backups anytime
- Import animals via CSV for easy migration
- All data stored locally on your device

Whether you raise beef cattle, dairy goats, laying hens, or a diverse mix of livestock, Steady Hand helps you stay organized and make informed decisions for your operation.

Download now and take control of your farm records.
